Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The check and the report are free with no sign-up. If you want help fixing things, you can ask for a free plan and a quote, and you decide from there.

Where do the results come from?

From Google’s own testing tool, Lighthouse, run through Google PageSpeed Insights. We translate its technical output into plain language and rank the problems for you.

Why does my score change each time I check?

Each check loads your site fresh, and small things vary: how busy your server is, ads, and network conditions. Scores moving by a few points is normal. Big problems show up every time.

Do you store my website or my results?

No. The check runs in your browser and nothing is saved on our side. Your address is only sent to us if you ask for a fix plan.

My site was built with Wix, Squarespace or WordPress. Can it still be fixed?

Usually, yes. Many fixes, like resizing images or writing page descriptions, can be done in your site editor. The report marks which ones you can do yourself.
